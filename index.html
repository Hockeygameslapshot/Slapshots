HOW TO PLAY

3-minute games, 1-minute sudden-death overtime, then a shootout. Win games to earn trophies and climb through 12 arenas. Your first win each day earns a card pack: gold, player cards for upgrades, or a new legend.

🎮 CONTROLS
  • FACEOFF: tap any button the instant the puck drops. Too early and you lose it. Aim the pad to choose where you draw it.
  • PAD skate. You always control the skater with the gold ring. On a computer: WASD or arrows, Space shoots, Shift dekes, E passes, Esc pauses.
  • SHOOT hold to wind up, let go to fire. Push the pad left or right to pick a corner. Push it toward the net as you let go to go top shelf. A quick tap is a wrist shot.
  • ONE-TIMER: tap SHOOT the moment a pass reaches you.
  • PASS goes to the open teammate, or the one you're pointing at. Teammates show their job above them; gold OPEN! means they're in a scoring spot with a clean lane.
  • DEKE side-steps and drags the puck across. Point the pad left or right to choose the side. Pokes and checks can't touch you mid-deke.
  • BACKHAND: tap SHOOT right after a deke, or while skating away from the net, for an instant shot the goalie reads late.
  • BREAKAWAY: get past every defender and the goalie comes out to challenge. Deke him and finish.
  • REBOUNDS: goalies kick a lot of saves back into the slot. Crash the net and bury the second chance (REBOUND GOAL!).
  • On defence: POKE reaches out with the stick, CHECK lunges for a body hit, SWITCH changes skaters.
  • YOUR GOALIE: when your goalie gets the puck, YOU control him. Skate him around his crease (he can't leave it), then SHOOT or PASS it up. Hold it too long with someone on you and he covers it for a whistle.
  • PULL THE GOALIE (🥅 button at the top, or G on a computer): your goalie goes to the bench and an extra attacker jumps on, 4 skaters against 3, but your net is EMPTY. Tap it again to put him back. He comes back on his own after any goal. The CPU pulls theirs too when they're down late.
  • ICING: clear the puck from your half past their goal line and the faceoff comes back to your zone. OFFSIDE: the puck must cross the blue line before you do.
  • EMOTES: tap the speech bubble on the rink's edge (or T, then 1-9). You start with 3 and find more in Epic, Mythic and Legendary card packs.
🥅 SHOOTOUT
  • Three shooters each, then sudden death. One chance per attempt, no rebounds.
  • When you shoot: skate in, deke and shoot like normal.
  • When the CPU shoots, you're the goalie. The pad moves him (side to side, and out to cut the angle). Tap SAVE just as he shoots to read it: lightning reflexes for half a second, and a ROBBED! save. Tap too early and you're down in the butterfly when he dekes. DIVE (the pass button) lunges left or right to cover the far post. POKE poke checks when he walks in close (half work, more when he's right on top of you, and almost always if you catch him faking).
  • Scouting report: before each CPU shot you see what kind of shooter is coming. \u{1F3AF} SNIPER shoots high from out: square up and read it. \u{1F4A5} POWER rips it early and low. \u{1F300} DANGLER dekes in close: stay up, don't read too early. \u{1F3AD} TRICKSTER fakes a shot then dekes: poke him during the fake! Stop 2 in a row for a streak, and at the end you see how many you stopped.
  • \u{1F9E4} GOALIE CHALLENGE (Arcade): you're in net for 10 CPU shooters, each one better than the last. Stop 6 to beat it.
🃏 YOUR CARDS
  • UPGRADES: 10 copies of a player + gold raises one attribute a level (+6%, up to level 5). Open a card in CARDS to upgrade it.
  • RATINGS: every attribute is a number from 40 to 99. A plain player is 60, each level adds 6, maxed is 90, and a legend's natural boost goes higher (up to 99). The coloured badge on every card is his OVERALL (only the Ultra Legend, Damien Shark, can reach 99 overall when maxed; everyone else tops out at 98). Each card also shows how fast the CPU skaters in your arena are.
  • WHAT EACH ONE DOES: SPEED = top skating speed. ACCELERATION = how quickly he gets to top speed. DEKING = puck handling and dekes. PASSING = pass speed and how clean he catches them. DEFENSIVE = poke checks and hits. Goalies: REFLEXES, SIDE TO SIDE, REBOUNDS.
  • MAXED PERKS: maxing some attributes unlocks a bonus. PASSING: every pass to him is caught clean. DEFENSIVE: 3 out of 4 of his poke checks work (a deke still beats them). REBOUNDS (goalie): he holds on to 9 out of 10 saves.
  • COMPARE: open any card and pick another player to see them side by side, or tap 📊 COMPARE ALL YOUR PLAYERS for one big table (tap a column to sort, tap a player to open him).
  • MASTERY: every player has his own mastery. Play games with him (CPU games and weekly challenges) to earn points: playing, winning, goals, assists, hits and takeaways (goalies: saves and shutouts). 9 levels, Bronze I to Gold III (MASTER), each with a reward you claim on his card.
  • NEW PLAYERS: when a card pack brings someone new, tap MEET YOUR NEW PLAYER. Tap the mystery card 3 times to scout him, guess who it is (right = +100 gold), and watch him sign.
  • \u{1F988} DAMIEN SHARK (ULTRA LEGEND): when you get him you pick his position for good: LW, RW, C, LD, RD or G. Centre = FACEOFF KING + perfect-passing super. Wingers = off-wing one-timers + a one-timer bomb super. Defence = SMELLS BLOOD + FEEDING FRENZY. Goalie = JAWS (no rebounds). Find him in Legendary card packs.
  • SUPERS (Arena 3+): only LEGENDS have supers, each based on a real NHL star's signature move (like Halvorsen's THE OFFICE, inspired by Ovechkin, or Okafor's OPEN-ICE HIT, which sends someone off the ice). Tap SUPERS on the home screen to see what every one does. Uncommon and regular players don't have one. The purple SUPER button fills up as you play (faster when you shoot or land hits). When it says READY, tap it (C on a computer). The CPU always plays the same kind of deck as you (a legend for each of your legends, an uncommon for each uncommon) but with different players, and from Arena 3 up their legends use supers too.
🏆 TROPHIES & ARENAS
  • TROPHIES: under 1000, a win is +30 and a loss is -15 (-10 in overtime or a shootout). Past 1000 it gets much harder: wins are worth 20 (dropping to 10 by 4000) and losses cost 20 to 25.
  • TROPHY ROADS: 3 on 3 and 5 on 5 each have their own trophies and road, with a prize every 100 trophies all the way to 10,000.
  • ARENAS: 12 arenas. Every arena is a bigger rink than the last (arena 1 is the smallest), with more open ice.
  • THE CPU gets better the more trophies you have: smarter, better hands, better goalies. Its skating speed tops out about the same as one of your maxed players.
  • 5 ON 5: switch between 3 ON 3 and 5 ON 5 above the PLAY button. In 5 on 5 each team dresses a C, LW, RW, LD and RD plus a goalie. Your deck plays C, a wing and D; two depth players fill the other wing and D.
  • RANKS (SOCIAL): leaderboards for trophies, plus a PLAYERS board for the most wins, points, goals or assists with each card.
⚡ EVENTS
  • DAILY QUESTS: 3 quests a day plus 1 player quest (score, assist, hit or poke with one of your players). Claim the rewards in Quests. Quests count in CPU games.
  • WEEKLY CHALLENGE: 15 wins before 3 losses with special rules. You keep every reward you reach. Each challenge can only be beaten once; a new one starts every Monday.
  • 🕹️ ARCADE: play special modes against the CPU any time, just for fun (no trophies, pucks, quests or mastery): Shootout, Sudden Death, First to 5, Empty Nets, Super Frenzy, Maxed Battle, Mystery Legends, the obstacle rinks below, and these:
  • \u{1F3C1} FASTEST SKATER: just you. Skate one lap through the numbered gates (yellow = next, green = finish) as fast as you can. Your fastest players (SPEED and ACCEL) post the best times. Your best lap is saved.
  • \u{1F3D2} BREAKAWAY CHALLENGE: 10 breakaways in a row on their goalie, rotating through your players. Score 6 to beat it. Best score saved.
  • \u{1F525} SURVIVAL: you start up 3-0 and there's no clock. Every 30 seconds the CPU skaters get faster and sharper. When they tie it, it's over. How long can you last?
  • \u23F1\uFE0F POWER PLAY SURVIVAL: you're a skater short the whole game (2 on 3), but every goal you score counts for 2.
  • \u{1F7E0} DOUBLE PUCK: a second, orange puck is loose. Nobody can carry it: skate into it and it fires at the other net. Goals count the same.
  • \u{1F319} MOON ICE: almost no friction. You take forever to speed up and stop, and the puck keeps sliding.
  • OBSTACLE RINKS (3 on 3, biggest rink): 🪨 ROCKS are solid, so bank passes off them (a lifted puck flies over). 🌨️ SLUSH slows skaters and the puck. 🟣 BUMPERS fling the puck and skaters. 🚀 SPEED PADS launch you down the ice. 🚜 ICE MACHINES drive across the neutral zone. 🧱 ICE WALLS block lanes. 🌀 PORTALS: shoot into one and it pops out the other. CHAOS RINK has a bit of everything. Both ends always get the same layout.
👥 FRIENDS
  • FRIENDLY BATTLES: in FRIENDS, pick a mode at the top (3 on 3, 5 on 5, Shootout, Sudden Death, Maxed Battle, Super Frenzy, Mystery Legends, First to 5, Empty Nets, Moon Ice, Double Puck or any obstacle rink), then tap PLAY next to a friend, or host with a code. Your friend sees which mode it is. No trophies either way.
  • WATCH: tap WATCH next to a friend who's in a game to see it live.
🎬 CLIPS
  • After a goal, tap 🎬 SAVE CLIP to save the replay as a video you can post. Or pause and tap ⏺ Record to record your gameplay, and tap it again to stop.
SUPERS
Unlock in Arena 3

Shot supers (🎯 The Office, 💣 One-Timer Bomb, ☄️ 108 MPH Slapper) work on your next shot: tap SUPER and it says NEXT SHOT = THE OFFICE, then shoot like normal. Or hold SUPER, drag to aim (up = top shelf) and let go to fire right away. On a computer: tap C, or hold C and aim with A/D. Only legends have supers, and each one is based on a real NHL player's signature move. Uncommon and regular players don't get one. The purple SUPER button fills up as you play (faster when you shoot or hit). When it says READY, tap it (C on a computer). The super belongs to whoever you're controlling.
